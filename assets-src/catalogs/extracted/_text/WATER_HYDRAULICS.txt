JANUS PRODUCT RANGE
P U M P S 
The Janus range of pumps are based on the axial piston 
principle which is both light and compact relative to their 
volumetric displacement, which may be altered in our 
variable displacement models. Up to 9 pistons per pump 
ensure very small pressure ripples removing the need for 
accumulators. The need for oil lubrication is eliminated as 
all moving parts are water lubricated. The pumps are 
manufactured in 316 or duplex materials making them 
highly resistant to corrosion and ideal for reverse osmosis 
applications.
