Welcome to our range of Helical Rotary Actuators, designed 
to deliver high performance in demanding mobile and 
industrial applications.
Thanks to their internal construction with advanced 
technology, these actuators ensure precise, compact, 
and powerful motion minimizing space requirements and 
maintenance.
In this brochure, you will find a selection of models tailored 
to different needs, with customizable solutions to optimize 
every process.
Reliability, efficiency and innovation are the pillars of our 
offer, ensuring top performances in each application: 
construction, mining, agricultural, marine, nautical, 
industrial, aerial working platforms, cranes, road 
equipments, garbage collection and many others.
Explore our range and find the perfect actuator suitable for 
your needs!
